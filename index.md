## Overview

Block 3.5 is different from other blocks in BIMS 6000. Rather than focusing on specific biological content, the goal is to build hands-on proficiency with computational tools that are indispensable for modern biomedical research:

- Accessing UVA's High-Performance Computing (HPC) system
- Navigating and manipulating files in the UNIX shell
- Basic programming in R and RStudio
- End-to-end RNA-seq data analysis

Students arrive with a broad range of computational experience. We do not expect proficiency after four days — we hope this introduction makes you comfortable reaching for these tools in your own research.
